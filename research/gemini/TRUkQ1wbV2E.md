### 6) WHY THE ENDING LANDS
The ending works because of **situational irony**. The scammers are "too smart" for their own good, selling a mine right before reaching a fortune. It provides a satisfying "justice" where the unsuspecting buyer wins through pure luck, contrasted against the 10 years of wasted effort by the "clever" miner. The visual of the "10ft" measurement provides concrete proof of how close they were to success.
