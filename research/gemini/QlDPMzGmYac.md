### 6) WHY THE ENDING LANDS

The ending succeeds through **Physics Irony**. The rival’s sabotage unintentionally saved the protagonist by moving the dangerous curve to the slowest part of the ride. The visual of a massive nuclear-style explosion for the rival provides a satisfyingly exaggerated "instant karma" payoff.
