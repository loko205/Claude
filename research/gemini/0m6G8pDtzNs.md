### 6) WHY THE ENDING LANDS
The ending succeeds by flipping the "dream job" (getting paid to do nothing) into a psychological horror. The final visual of the protagonist looking trapped emphasizes that isolation is a more effective "firing" method than a pink slip, providing a chilling resolution to the mystery of the disappeared coworker.
