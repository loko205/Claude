### 4. Comparison to "Ricks Clips" Opening
A Ricks Clips opening usually features:
*   **Instant Absurdity:** A character is usually *already* in a high-stress or bizarre situation (e.g., falling, screaming, or holding something impossible).
*   **Proximity:** The character's face/expression is usually much larger in the frame to establish "character" immediately.
*   **Moving Camera:** Ricks uses constant "virtual handheld" shake or aggressive pans.
*   **This video is weaker because:** It starts with an "Establishing Shot." Establishing shots are for movies; Shorts require "In Media Res" (starting in the middle of the action). Starting with a static engineer is "dead air."
