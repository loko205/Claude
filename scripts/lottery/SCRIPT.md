## Facts (verified via web search, 2026-11-25)
- Jerry & Marge Selbee, retired couple from Evart, Michigan. Jerry ran a convenience store there, has a math degree.
- Michigan "Winfall": when the jackpot hit the roll-down level ($5M in Michigan) and nobody matched all 6 numbers, the
  jackpot was split among the lower-tier winners -> each ticket worth more than it cost.
- He worked out the trick within minutes of reading the game's rules (widely reported: "found in minutes").
- First big buy: $3,600 of tickets -> won $6,300 (60 Minutes; NY Post calls it the 2nd try).
- Later played Massachusetts' near-identical Cash WinFall, printing tickets for up to 12-hour days at small stores.
- 9 years, gross ~$26–27M, ~$7.75M pre-tax profit (Boston Globe figures). Ended when the game was discontinued (2012).
Sources: cbsnews.com 60 Minutes (2019-06-09), finance.yahoo.com "How A Couple Won $26 Million…", Wikipedia
